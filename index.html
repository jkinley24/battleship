RECRUIT

EASY MODE

Fires at completely random coordinates each turn with no memory or strategy. Every untried cell has an equal chance of being selected, making this opponent forgiving and unpredictable. Ideal for learning the ropes.

⊕

CAPTAIN

MEDIUM MODE

Uses a Hunt & Target algorithm. Fires randomly until scoring a hit, then switches to target mode — probing adjacent cells and locking onto the ship's orientation to sink it efficiently. A balanced challenge that rewards smart placement.

⚡

ADMIRAL

HARD MODE

Employs Probability Density mapping on top of Hunt & Target. Every turn it calculates how many remaining ships could legally occupy each cell and fires at the highest-probability square. A ruthless tactician.
